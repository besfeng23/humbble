## 1. Purpose & Copy Governance Rules

Every user-facing string in the repository (`README.md`, `app/`, `components/`, `DB/`) and every planned screen (`SCR-01` through `SCR-24`) is subject to the following binding copy guardrails:

1. **Neutral Shared-Interest Positioning Only (`Alternative A` / `RA 10906`):**
   - All public and in-app copy must describe Humbble neutrally as an adult (`18+`) shared-interest dating and personal-connection platform in the Philippines.
   - **Strictly Prohibited:** Any marriage-broker, mail-order spouse, catalogue, or nationality-pairing marketing (e.g., *"Meet Filipina Wives"*, *"Foreign Men Seeking Filipino Brides"*, *"International Spouse Catalogue"*, or `"marriage"` filter tags).
2. **Zero Unsupported or Misleading Claims (`RA 7394` / `RA 10173`):**
   - Never claim *"No tracking"* or *"Zero PII"* when pseudonymous analytics (`PostHog`) or session/security logs are used; describe telemetry accurately as **opt-in PII-minimized pseudonymous analytics**.
   - Never advertise unbuilt features (*"Spotlight for 30 minutes"*, *"Get exclusive photo insights"*, *"Two compliments a weeks"*, or two paid tiers `"Premium+"` vs `"Premium"`).
   - Never present pilot performance targets (e.g., `≥95%` safety report triage in 24h, `≥99.5%` crash-free sessions, `≥70%` onboarding completion) as existing performance facts.
   - Never present an unverified business name (`RED-APPLE TECHNOLOGY AND DIGITAL SERVICES`) as an established legal entity until verified by the Owner and Philippine counsel (`DEP-LEGAL-02`).
